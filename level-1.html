Lesson 1

主题 (zhǔtí) Subject

问候语及自我介绍 Wènhòu yǔ jí zì wǒ jièshào Greetings & self-introduction

词语 (cíyǔ) Words

中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
我 wǒ I, me pronoun 我学习中文。 (Wǒ xuéxí Zhōngwén.) I study Chinese.  
你 nǐ you pronoun 你叫什么名字？ (Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?) What is your name?  
他 tā he, him pronoun 他是我的哥哥。 (Tā shì wǒ de gēge.) He is my older brother.  
她 tā she, her pronoun 她很聪明。 (Tā hěn cōngmíng.) She is very smart.  
它 tā it pronoun 它是一只猫。 (Tā shì yī zhī māo.) It is a cat.  
您好 nín hǎo hello (polite) phrase 您好，老师！ (Nín hǎo) lǎoshī! A respectful greeting. Use with elders, superiors, or in formal situations.
你好 nǐ hǎo hello phrase 你好，朋友！ (Nǐ hǎo) péngyou! Standard, informal greeting.
叫 jiào to be called, to call verb 我叫小明。 (Wǒ jiào Xiǎomíng.) My name is Xiaoming.  
你呢？ nǐ ne? and you? phrase 我很好，你呢？ (Wǒ hěn hǎo) nǐ ne?  
姓 xìng surname, family name noun, (verb) 你姓什么？ (Nǐ xìng shénme?) What is your surname? As a verb, it means "to have the surname of".
名 míng given name, first name noun 他的名字很好听。 (Tā de míngzi hěn hǎotīng.) His given name is very pleasant-sounding.  
谢谢 xièxie thank you, thanks verb / interjection 谢谢你的帮助。 (Xièxie nǐ de bāngzhù.) Thank you for your help.  
再见 zàijiàn goodbye, see you again verb / interjection 我们明天再见。 (Wǒmen míngtiān zàijiàn.) We'll see each other again tomorrow.  

句式结构 (jù shì jiégòu) Sentence structures

你叫什么（名）？ Nǐ jiào shénme (míng)? What is your name?

我叫 …… 。 Wǒ jiào ….. . I am called/named ….. .

你好吗？ Nǐ hǎo ma? How are you?

我很好。你呢？ Wǒ hěn hǎo. Nǐ ne? I am very good/well. And you?

Formula: Declarative sentence + 吗？

你好！ Nǐ hǎo! Hello!

我叫 … 。你叫什么？（你呢？） Wǒ jiào … . Nǐ jiào shénme? (Nǐ ne?) My name is … . What is your name? (And yours?)

我叫 … 。 Wǒ jiào … . My name is … .

你好吗？ Nǐ hǎo ma? How are you?

我很好。谢谢。 Wǒ hěn hǎo. Xièxiè. I am good. Thank you.

谢谢。 Xièxiè. Thank you.

Lesson 2

主题 (zhǔtí) Subject

数字和日期 Shùzì hé rìqí Numbers and dates

词语 (cíyǔ) Words

Table 1: 数字 (shùzì) numbers
中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
零 líng zero, 0 number 今天是零度。 (Jīntiān shì líng dù.) It is zero degrees today. Also means "zero" in temperature, scores, etc.
一 yī one, 1 number 我有一个苹果。 (Wǒ yǒu yī gè píngguǒ.) I have one apple. Tone changes to yì before 4th tone (e.g., 一个 yí gè).
二 èr two, 2 number 我有两个弟弟。 (Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè dìdi.) I have two younger brothers. For counting, 两 (liǎng) is often used instead of 二.
三 sān three, 3 number 现在三点。 (Xiànzài sān diǎn.) It's three o'clock now.  
四 sì four, 4 number 我家有四口人。 (Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén.) There are four people in my family.  
五 wǔ five, 5 number 星期五我有课。 (Xīngqīwǔ wǒ yǒu kè.) I have class on Friday.  
六 liù six, 6 number 我六点回家。 (Wǒ liù diǎn huí jiā.) I go home at six o'clock.  
七 qī seven, 7 number 七月很热。 (Qī yuè hěn rè.) July is very hot.  
八 bā eight, 8 number 我八月去北京。 (Wǒ bā yuè qù Běijīng.) I will go to Beijing in August.  
九 jiǔ nine, 9 number 九月开学。 (Jiǔ yuè kāixué.) School starts in September.  
十 shí ten, 10 number 这个孩子十岁。 (Zhège háizi shí suì.) This child is ten years old.  
百 bǎi hundred, 100 number 这本书一百块。 (Zhè běn shū yī bǎi kuài.) This book costs one hundred yuan. For numbers like 200, say 两百 (liǎng bǎi).
Table 2: 日期 (rìqí) dates
中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
年 nián year noun 我学习中文一年了。 (Wǒ xuéxí Zhōngwén yī nián le.) I have been studying Chinese for a year.  
月 yuè month, moon noun 这个月很忙。 (Zhège yuè hěn máng.) This month is very busy. Can mean both "moon" and "month".
日 rì day, date (formal) noun 今天是九月十日。 (Jīntiān shì jiǔ yuè shí rì.) Today is September 10th. More formal for dates; often used in writing.
号 hào date, number noun 今天是九月十号。 (Jīntiān shì jiǔ yuè shí hào.) Today is September 10th. More colloquial for dates; also means "number".
昨天 zuótiān yesterday noun 昨天是星期一。 (Zuótiān shì xīngqīyī.) Yesterday was Monday.  
今天 jīntiān today noun 今天天气很好。 (Jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.) The weather is very nice today.  
明天 míngtiān tomorrow noun 明天是我的生日。 (Míngtiān shì wǒ de shēngrì.) Tomorrow is my birthday.  
是 shì to be verb 我是学生。 (Wǒ shì xuésheng.) I am a student. The verb "to be" for nouns, not adjectives.
一月 yīyuè January noun 一月很冷。 (Yīyuè hěn lěng.) January is very cold. Months are written without a space.
二月 èryuè February noun 二月有二十八天。 (Èryuè yǒu èrshíbā tiān.) February has twenty-eight days.  
三月 sānyuè March noun 三月春天开始。 (Sānyuè chūntiān kāishǐ.) Spring starts in March.  
四月 sìyuè April noun 四月经常下雨。 (Sìyuè jīngcháng xià yǔ.) It often rains in April.  
五月 wǔyuè May noun 五月一日是劳动节。 (Wǔyuè yī rì shì láodòng jié.) May 1st is Labor Day.  
六月 liùyuè June noun 六月天气很热。 (Liùyuè tiānqì hěn rè.) The weather is very hot in June.  
七月 qīyuè July noun 七月是暑假。 (Qīyuè shì shǔjià.) July is summer vacation.  
八月 bāyuè August noun 八月我去了中国。 (Bāyuè wǒ qùle Zhōngguó.) I went to China in August.  
九月 jiǔyuè September noun 九月学校开学。 (Jiǔyuè xuéxiào kāixué.) School starts in September.  
十月 shíyuè October noun 十月有国庆节。 (Shíyuè yǒu guóqìng jié.) October has National Day.  
十一月 shíyīyuè November noun 十一月天气变冷了。 (Shíyīyuè tiānqì biàn lěng le.) The weather gets colder in November. Months 11 and 12 should be written as single words.
十二月 shí'èryuè December noun 十二月是最后一个月。 (Shí'èryuè shì zuìhòu yīgè yuè.) December is the last month. There's often a light pause between "shí" and "èr", marked by an apostrophe.

Lesson 3

主题 (zhǔtí) Subject

生日 Shēngrì Birthday

词语 (cíyǔ) Words

中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
生日 shēngrì birthday noun 明天是我的生日。 (Míngtiān shì wǒ de shēngrì.) Tomorrow is my birthday.  
面条 miàntiáo noodles noun 我喜欢吃面条。 (Wǒ xǐhuān chī miàntiáo.) I like to eat noodles. Often refers to wheat noodles.
钱 qián money noun 我没有钱。 (Wǒ méiyǒu qián.) I don't have money.  
卡 kǎ card noun 这是我的银行卡。 (Zhè shì wǒ de yínháng kǎ.) This is my bank card. Short for 卡片 (kǎpiàn).
歌 gē song noun 他会唱很多歌。 (Tā huì chàng hěnduō gē.) He can sing many songs.  
礼物 lǐwù gift, present noun 谢谢你的礼物。 (Xièxie nǐ de lǐwù.) Thank you for your gift.  
蛋糕 dàngāo cake noun 我买了一个蛋糕。 (Wǒ mǎile yī gè dàngāo.) I bought a cake.  
多大 duō dà how old phrase 你多大了？ (Nǐ duō dà le?) How old are you? Used to ask age. There is a space in pinyin.
..岁 .. suì .. years old phrase 我二十岁。 (Wǒ èrshí suì.) I am twenty years old.  
生日礼物 shēngrì lǐwù birthday gift noun 这是我给你的生日礼物。 (Zhè shì wǒ gěi nǐ de shēngrì lǐwù.) This is the birthday gift I give you.  
生日蛋糕 shēngrì dàngāo birthday cake noun 我们吃生日蛋糕吧。 (Wǒmen chī shēngrì dàngāo ba.) Let's eat the birthday cake.  
生日歌 shēngrì gē birthday song noun 我们唱生日歌。 (Wǒmen chàng shēngrì gē.) We sang the birthday song. Often refers to "Happy Birthday to You".
生日卡 shēngrì kǎ birthday card noun 我收到一张生日卡。 (Wǒ shōudào yī zhāng shēngrì kǎ.) I received a birthday card.  
的 de (possessive/attributive particle) particle 这是我的书。 (Zhè shì wǒ de shū.) This is my book. Used to indicate possession or attribution.
我的 wǒ de my, mine pronoun phrase 我的家在北京。 (Wǒ de jiā zài Běijīng.) My home is in Beijing.  
他的 tā de his pronoun phrase 他的车是新的。 (Tā de chē shì xīn de.) His car is new.  
她的 tā de her, hers pronoun phrase 她的名字是小红。 (Tā de míngzi shì Xiǎohóng.) Her name is Xiaohong.  

句子 (jùzi) Sentences

你的生日是几月几号？ Nǐ de shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào? When is your birthday?

我的生日是一月二十日。 Wǒ de shēngrì shì yī yuè èrshí rì. His/her birthday is on 20th of January.

他/她的生日是几月几日？ Tā de shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ rì? When is his/her birthday?

他/她的生日是一月二十号。 Tā de shēngrì shì yī yuè èrshí hào. His/her birthday is on 20th of January.

祝你生日快乐！ Zhù nǐ shēngrì kuài lè! Happy birthday to you!

Lesson 4

主题 (zhǔtí) Subject

年龄、星期几和时间 Niánlíng, xīngqí jǐ hé shíjiān Age, days of the week and time

词语 (cíyǔ) Words

Table 3: 星期 (xīngqī) days of the week
中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
星期 xīngqī week noun 一个星期有七天。 (Yī gè xīngqī yǒu qī tiān.) There are seven days in a week.  
天 tiān day, sky noun 今天天气很好。 (Jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.) The weather is very nice today. Means both "day" and "sky".
星期一 xīngqīyī Monday noun 星期一我很忙。 (Xīngqīyī wǒ hěn máng.) I am very busy on Monday. Days of the week are written as single words.
星期二 xīngqī'èr Tuesday noun 星期二有中文课。 (Xīngqī'èr yǒu Zhōngwén kè.) There is Chinese class on Tuesday.  
星期三 xīngqīsān Wednesday noun 星期三我们考试。 (Xīngqīsān wǒmen kǎoshì.) We have an exam on Wednesday.  
星期四 xīngqīsì Thursday noun 星期四我去看电影。 (Xīngqīsì wǒ qù kàn diànyǐng.) I go to watch a movie on Thursday.  
星期五 xīngqīwǔ Friday noun 星期五晚上我休息。 (Xīngqīwǔ wǎnshàng wǒ xiūxi.) I rest on Friday evening.  
星期六 xīngqīliù Saturday noun 星期六是周末。 (Xīngqīliù shì zhōumò.) Saturday is the weekend.  
星期天 xīngqītiān Sunday noun 星期天我在家。 (Xīngqītiān wǒ zài jiā.) I am at home on Sunday. 星期天 is more common in spoken language.
星期日 xīngqīrì Sunday noun 星期日商店开门吗？ (Xīngqīrì shāngdiàn kāimén ma?) Is the store open on Sunday? 星期日 is slightly more formal.
Table 4: 时间 (shíjiān) time
中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
昨天 zuótiān yesterday noun 昨天天气不好。 (Zuótiān tiānqì bù hǎo.) The weather was bad yesterday.  
明天 míngtiān tomorrow noun 明天见。 (Míngtiān jiàn.) See you tomorrow.  
上班 shàng bān to go to work, to start work verb 我八点上班。 (Wǒ bā diǎn shàng bān.) I go to work at eight o'clock. Pinyin has a space.
下班 xià bān to get off work, to finish work verb 我五点下班。 (Wǒ wǔ diǎn xià bān.) I get off work at five o'clock. Pinyin has a space.
点 diǎn o'clock, hour noun 现在三点。 (Xiànzài sān diǎn.) It's three o'clock now.  
几点 jǐ diǎn what time phrase 现在几点？ (Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?) What time is it now?  
半 bàn half noun 现在是三点半。 (Xiànzài shì sān diǎn bàn.) It's three-thirty now.  
一刻 yī kè a quarter (15 minutes) noun 八点一刻是8:15。 (Bā diǎn yī kè shì 8:15.) Eight-fifteen is 8:15.  
…差一刻 … chà yī kè a quarter to … phrase 十点差一刻是9:45。 (Shí diǎn chà yī kè shì 9:45.) A quarter to ten is 9:45. The word is 差 (chà), not 叉.
…又一刻 … yòu yī kè … and a quarter phrase 七点又一刻是7:15。 (Qī diǎn yòu yī kè shì 7:15.) Seven and a quarter is 7:15. This structure is less common than "七点一刻".
现在 xiànzài now noun 现在几点了？ (Xiànzài jǐ diǎn le?) What time is it now?  
分（钟） fēn (zhōng) minute noun 火车晚点三分钟。 (Huǒchē wǎndiǎn sān fēnzhōng.) The train is three minutes late. 分钟 (fēnzhōng) is the full word.
时间 shíjiān time noun 我没有时间。 (Wǒ méiyǒu shíjiān.) I don't have time.  
上午 shàngwǔ morning, a.m. noun 我上午上课。 (Wǒ shàngwǔ shàngkè.) I have class in the morning.  
中午 zhōngwǔ noon noun 我们中午吃饭。 (Wǒmen zhōngwǔ chīfàn.) We eat at noon.  
下午 xiàwǔ afternoon, p.m. noun 下午三点开会。 (Xiàwǔ sān diǎn kāihuì.) The meeting is at three in the afternoon.  
晚上 wǎnshàng evening, night noun 我晚上看电视。 (Wǒ wǎnshàng kàn diànshì.) I watch TV in the evening.  
七点半 qī diǎn bàn seven-thirty, half past seven phrase 电影七点半开始。 (Diànyǐng qī diǎn bàn kāishǐ.) The movie starts at seven-thirty.  
七点一刻 qī diǎn yī kè seven-fifteen, a quarter past seven phrase 我们七点一刻见面。 (Wǒmen qī diǎn yī kè jiànmiàn.) Let's meet at seven-fifteen. This is a more common way to say "quarter past".

句子 (jùzi) Sentences

Formula: subject + number + 岁(suì)。

你多大？/ 你几岁？ Nǐ duōdà? Nǐ jǐ suì? How old are you?

我二十五岁。 Wǒ èrshíwǔ suì. I am 25 years old.

今天是星期几？ Jīntiān shì xīngqī jǐ? What day is it today?

今天是星期五。 Jīntiān shì xīngqī wǔ. It is Friday today.

现在几点？ Xiànzài jǐ diǎn? What time is it now?

现在七点。 Xiànzài qī diǎn. It is seven o'clock.

你几点下课？ Nǐ jǐdiǎn xià kè? When do you finish the lesson?

我八点半下课。 Wǒ bā diǎn bàn xià kè. I finish the lesson at 8:30pm.

Note: the preposition “at” ect. in English, when it is used before time, it does not need to be translated in Chinese. So does the word do/did etc. in the questions in English.

Lesson 5

主题 (zhǔtí) Subject

时间和职业 Shíjiān hé zhíyè Time and occupation

词语 - 时间 (cíyǔ - shíjiān) Words - time

Table 5: 时间 (shíjiān) time
中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
一点 yī diǎn one o'clock phrase 会议在一点开始。 (Huìyì zài yī diǎn kāishǐ.) The meeting starts at one o'clock. For time on the hour: [Number] + 点.
两点十分 liǎng diǎn shí fēn ten past two (2:10) phrase 火车两点十分到站。 (Huǒchē liǎng diǎn shí fēn dào zhàn.) The train arrives at ten past two. Format: [Number] + 点 + [Number] + 分.
三点三十五分 sān diǎn sānshí wǔ fēn twenty-five to four (3:35) phrase 现在是三点三十五分。 (Xiànzài shì sān diǎn sānshí wǔ fēn.) It's now twenty-five to four. For minutes > 10, use the full number (三十五).
四点五分 sì diǎn wǔ fēn five past four (4:05) phrase 飞机四点五分起飞。 (Fēijī sì diǎn wǔ fēn qǐfēi.) The plane takes off at five past four. For single-digit minutes, add a leading 零 (líng) in more formal speech (四点零五分).
七点一刻 qī diǎn yī kè quarter past seven (7:15) phrase 电影七点一刻开始。 (Diànyǐng qī diǎn yī kè kāishǐ.) The movie starts at a quarter past seven. A common way to express 15 minutes past.
七点半 qī diǎn bàn half past seven (7:30) phrase 我们七点半吃饭。 (Wǒmen qī diǎn bàn chīfàn.) We eat dinner at half past seven. 半 (bàn) means "half".
七点四十五分 qī diǎn sìshí wǔ fēn quarter to eight (7:45) phrase 商店七点四十五分关门。 (Shāngdiàn qī diǎn sìshí wǔ fēn guānmén.) The store closes at a quarter to eight. Can also be expressed as 七点三刻 (qī diǎn sān kè) or 差一刻八点 (chà yī kè bā diǎn).
十二点二十分 shí'èr diǎn èrshí fēn twenty past twelve (12:20) phrase 我们十二点二十分见面。 (Wǒmen shí'èr diǎn èrshí fēn jiànmiàn.) Let's meet at twenty past twelve. For 12-hour format, add 上午/下午/晚上 for clarity.
Table 6: 职业 (zhíyè) occupation
中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (Cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzǐ) Sentence 笔记 (Bǐjì) Note
职业 zhíyè occupation, profession noun 你的职业是什么？ (Nǐ de zhíyè shì shénme?) What is your occupation? A formal term for one's line of work.
工作 gōngzuò work, job noun, verb 我在银行工作。 (Wǒ zài yínháng gōngzuò.) I work at a bank. Can be used as a noun (一份工作) or verb (去工作).
做 zuò to do, to make verb 你做什么工作？ (Nǐ zuò shénme gōngzuò?) What work do you do? A very common and versatile verb.
老板 lǎobǎn boss, proprietor noun 我的老板很忙。 (Wǒ de lǎobǎn hěn máng.) My boss is very busy.  
产品 chǎnpǐn product noun 我们公司生产电子产品。 (Wǒmen gōngsī shēngchǎn diànzǐ chǎnpǐn.) Our company manufactures electronic products.  
经理 jīnglǐ manager noun 他是公司的经理。 (Tā shì gōngsī de jīnglǐ.) He is the manager of the company.  
学生 xuéshēng student noun 我是北京大学的学生。 (Wǒ shì Běijīng Dàxué de xuéshēng.) I am a student at Peking University.  
工程师 gōngchéngshī engineer noun 我的爸爸是工程师。 (Wǒ de bàba shì gōngchéngshī.) My father is an engineer.  
教师 jiàoshī teacher noun 我的妈妈是教师。 (Wǒ de māma shì jiàoshī.) My mother is a teacher. A formal term; 老师 (lǎoshī) is more common.
医生 yīshēng doctor, physician noun 我想成为一名医生。 (Wǒ xiǎng chéngwéi yī míng yīshēng.) I want to become a doctor.  
全职 quánzhí full-time adjective 我有一份全职工作。 (Wǒ yǒu yī fèn quánzhí gōngzuò.) I have a full-time job. Often used as an attribute before nouns like 工作.

我下午六点半下班。 Wǒ xià wǔ liù diǎn bàn xià bān. I finish work at 6:30pm.
