Lesson 6

主题 (zhǔtí) Subject

第六棵：练习使用“被”字句。 Dì liù kè: Liànxí shǐyòng “bèi” zì jù.

词汇 (Cíhuì) Vocabulary

中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (cílèi) Parts of speech 句子 (jùzi) Sentence 词语短语 (cíyǔ duǎnyǔ) Words and phrases 笔记 (bǐjì) Note
借 jiè to borrow, to lend verb 我可以借你的书吗? (Wǒ kěyǐ jiè nǐ de shū ma?) Can I borrow your book? 借钱 (jièqián) borrow money, 借给 (jiègěi) lend to Context decides "borrow" or "lend".
拿 ná to hold, to take verb 请你拿这杯水给我。 (Qǐng nǐ ná zhè bēi shuǐ gěi wǒ.) Please hold this cup of water for me. 拿走 (názǒu) take away, 拿手 (náshǒu) skilled at "拿手" is an adjective meaning "expert/specialty".
踢 tī to kick, to play (soccer) verb 他踢足球踢得很好。 (Tā tī zúqiú tī de hěn hǎo.) He plays football very well. 踢开 (tī kāi) kick away, 踢球 (tī qiú) play football Verb is repeated before the "得" structure.
撞 zhuàng to bump, to hit verb 他不小心撞坏了自行车。 (Tā bù xiǎoxīn zhuàng huài le zìxíngchē.) He accidentally crashed and broke the bike. 撞伤 (zhuàngshāng) injure by hitting, 撞坏 (zhuànghuài) wreck/damage by hitting Use "撞伤" for people/animals, "撞坏" for objects.
咬 yǎo to bite verb 小狗把他咬哭了。 (Xiǎo gǒu bǎ tā yǎo kū le.) The puppy bit him and made him cry. 咬哭 (yǎokū) bite and make cry, 咬住 (yǎo zhù) hold tightly with teeth "咬哭" uses "哭" as a resultative complement.
打 dǎ to hit, to play (balls) verb 那个小男孩把镜子打破了。 (Nàge xiǎo nánhái bǎ jìngzi dǎ pò le.) That little boy broke the mirror. 打破 (dǎpò) break/shatter, 打哭 (dǎkū) hit and make cry "打破" is widely used metaphorically (e.g., break a record).
淋 lín to pour, to drench verb 突然下雨，我的衣服被淋湿了。 (Tūrán xià yǔ) wǒ de yīfu bèi lín shī le. 淋湿 (línshī) soaked wet, 淋雨 (lín yǔ) get rained on "淋湿" is the most common resultative pairing for rain.
抓 zhuā to catch, to grab verb 猫抓老鼠很快。 (Māo zhuā lǎoshǔ hěn kuài.) The cat catches mice quickly. 抓住 (zhuā zhù) catch hold of, 抓紧 (zhuā jǐn) hold tightly "抓紧" can metaphorically mean "to hurry up".
吹 chuī to blow, to boast verb 大风把外面的树吹倒了。 (Dàfēng bǎ wàimiàn de shù chuī dǎo le.) The strong wind blew down the tree outside. 吹倒 (chuīdǎo) blow down, 吹牛 (chuīniú) boast "吹倒" uses the directional/resultative "倒".
砍 kǎn to cut, to chop verb 工人们正在砍倒那棵枯树。 (Gōngrénmen zhèngzài kǎn dǎo nà kē kū shù.) The workers are chopping down that dead tree. 砍倒 (kǎndǎo) chop down, 砍价 (kǎn jià) bargain "砍价" is a highly common slang for haggling.
偷 tōu to steal; secretly verb, adverb 有人偷了我的钱包。 (Yǒu rén tōu le wǒ de qiңnbāo.) Someone stole my wallet. 偷东西 (tōu dōngxi) steal things, 偷看 (tōu kàn) peek/steal a glance (secretly)  
摔 shuāi to fall, to throw down verb 他不小心把杯子摔碎了。 (Tā bù xiǎoxīn bǎ bēizi shuāi suì le.) He accidentally dropped and shattered the cup. 摔碎 (shuāisuì) shatter by dropping, 摔倒 (shuāi dǎo) fall down "摔碎" implies the object broke into tiny pieces.
倒 dǎo to fall, to collapse verb, result complement 书倒在地地上。 (Shū dǎo zài dìshàng le.) The book fell on the floor. 倒下 (dǎo xià) fall down, 倒塌 (dǎotā) collapse Also pronounced "dào" when meaning "to pour".
坏 huài bad; broken adjective, result complement 我的手机被撞坏了。 (Wǒ de shǒujī bèi zhuàng huài le.) My phone was broken in a collision. 撞坏 (zhuànghuài) wreck by hitting, 坏掉 (huài diào) break down  
碎 suì broken, shattered adjective, result complement 他不小心把盘子打碎了。 (Tā bù xiǎoxīn bǎ pánzi dǎ suì le.) He accidentally smashed the plate. 打碎 (dǎsuì) smash/shatter, 摔碎 (shuāisuì) shatter by dropping Specifically describes items broken into small shards.
哭 kū to cry verb, result complement 妹妹被哥哥打哭了。 (Mèimei bèi gēge dǎ kū le.) The younger sister was hit and made to cry by her older brother. 打哭 (dǎkū) hit and make cry, 咬哭 (yǎokū) bite and make cry  
破 pò broken, torn adjective, result complement 我的衣服被树枝挂破了。 (Wǒ de yīfu bèi shùzhī guà pò le.) My clothes were torn by a tree branch. 打破 (dǎpò) break/shatter, 破坏 (pòhuài) destroy Used for torn fabrics, paper, or punctured surfaces.
伤 shāng to injure; injury verb, noun 他在车祸中撞伤了腿。 (Tā zài chēhuò zhōng zhuàng shāng le tuǐ.) He injured his leg in the car accident. 撞伤 (zhuàngshāng) injure by hitting, 受伤 (shòushāng) get injured "受伤" functions as a separable verb-object compound.
湿 shī wet, moist adjective 外面下大雨，我的鞋子全淋湿了。 (Wàimiàn xià dàyǔ) wǒ de xiézi quán lín shī le. 淋湿 (línshī) soaked wet, 弄湿 (nòng shī) get wet Frequently finishes with "了" to show a change of state.
断 duàn broken, snapped adjective, result complement 那只狗把绳子咬断了。 (Nà zhī gǒu bǎ shéngzi yǎo duàn le.) That dog bit and snapped the rope. 撞断 (zhuàngduàn) break by impact, 咬断 (yǎoduàn) snap by biting Used for elongated objects (ropes, bones, branches).
