东边 dōngbiān the east side noun 东边的商场 (dōngbiān de shāngcháng) the shopping mall on the east side, 学校东边 (xuéxiào dōngbiān) east of the school, 在银行东边 (zài yínháng dōngbiān) to the east of the bank 学校的东边有一个很大的体育馆。 (Xuéxiào de dōngbiān yǒu yī gè hěn dà de tǐyùguǎn.) There is a very large gymnasium on the east side of the school. Can be used with or without "的" depending on the noun modifying it.
东 dōng east noun 往东走 (wǎng dōng zǒu) walk toward the east, 东大门 (dōngdàmén) the east gate, 坐路车向东 (zuò lù chē xiàng dōng) take a bus heading east 你一直往东走，就能看到那个地铁站。 (Nǐ yīzhí wǎng dōng zǒu, jiù néng kàndào nàgè dìtiězhàn.) If you keep walking east, you will be able to see that subway station. Often paired with "往" (wǎng) or "向" (xiàng) to show direction.
离 lí away, off, from preposition 离学校 (lí xuéxiào) away from school, 离这儿 (lí zhè'r) away from here, 离那儿 (lí nà'r) away from there 我住的地方离大学有一点儿远。 (Wǒ zhù de dìfāng lí dàxué yǒu yīdiǎnr yuǎn.) The place where I live is a little far from the university. Used in the structure: "A 离 B + Distance/Adjective".
远 yuǎn far adjective 不远 (bù yuǎn) not far, 很远 (hěn yuǎn) very far, 非常远 (fēicháng yuǎn) extremely far, 太远 (tài yuǎn) too far, 离学院不太远 (lí xuéyuàn bù tài yuǎn) not too far from the college 超市离火车站不远，走几分钟就到了。 (Chāoshì lí huǒchēzhàn bù yuǎn, zǒu jǐ fēnzhōng jiù dào le.) The supermarket is not far from the train station, just a few minutes walk. Frequently negated as "不远" or modified by intensifiers like "很".
车站 chēzhàn bus stop, station noun 去车站 (qù chēzhàn) go to the station, 在车站等车 (zài chēzhàn děng chē) waiting for a bus at the station, 公交车站 (gōngjiāo chēzhàn) bus stop 前边那个车站有很多去市中心的公共汽车。 (Qiánbiān nàgè chēzhàn yǒu hěn duō qù shìzhōngxīn de gōnggòng qìchē.) That station ahead has many buses going to the city center. General term for bus stops, train stations, or subway stations.
前边 qiánbiān front side noun 前边的公园 (qiánbiān de gōngyuán) the park ahead, 前边的人 (qiánbiān de rén) the person in front, 花园小区前边 (huāyuán xiǎoqū qiánbiān) in front of the Huayuan neighborhood, 在宿舍前边 (zài sùshè qiánbiān) in front of the dormitory 请问，办公楼是不是在图书馆的前边？ (Qǐngwèn, bàngōnglóu shì bú shì zài túshūguǎn de qiánbiān?) Excuse me, is the office building in front of the library? Interchangeable with "前面" (qiánmiàn) in most spoken contexts.
前 qián front, forward noun 往前走 (wǎng qián zǒu) walk forward, 向前看 (xiàng qián kàn) look ahead, 前排 (qiánpái) front row 请大家一直往前走，不要在门口停下来。 (Qǐng dàjiā yīzhí wǎng qián zǒu, búyào zài ménkǒu tíng xiàlái.) Everyone please keep walking straight forward, do not stop at the doorway. Commonly acts as a directional target for movement verbs.
拐 guǎi to turn verb 往右拐 (wǎng yòu guǎi) turn right, 往左拐 (wǎng zuǒ guǎi) turn left, 往东拐 (wǎng dōng guǎi) turn east, 先往右拐 (xiān wǎng yòu guǎi) turn right first 在第一个十字路口往左拐\，就到美术系了。 (Zài dì yī gè shízìlùkǒu wǎng zuǒ guǎi, jiù dào měishùxì le.) Turn left at the first intersection, and you will arrive at the art department. "拐" is more colloquial; "转" (zhuǎn) is slightly more formal.

词汇 (Cíhuì) Vocabulary

中文 (zhōngwén) Chinese 拼音 (pīnyīn) Pinyin 英语 (yīngyǔ) English 词类 (cílèi) Parts of speech 词语短语 (cíyǔ duǎnyǔ) Words and phrases 句子 (jùzi) Sentence 笔记 (bǐjì) Note
借 jiè to borrow, to lend verb 借钱 (jièqián) borrow money, 借给 (jiègěi) lend to 我可以借你的书吗? (Wǒ kěyǐ jiè nǐ de shū ma?) Can I borrow your book? Context decides "borrow" or "lend".
拿 ná to hold, to take verb 拿走 (názǒu) take away, 拿手 (náshǒu) skilled at 请你拿这杯水给我。 (Qǐng nǐ ná zhè bēi shuǐ gěi wǒ.) Please hold this cup of water for me. "拿手" is an adjective meaning "expert/specialty".
踢 tī to kick, to play (soccer) verb 踢开 (tī kāi) kick away, 踢球 (tī qiú) play football 他踢足球踢得很好。 (Tā tī zúqiú tī de hěn hǎo.) He plays football very well. Verb is repeated before the "得" structure.
撞 zhuàng to bump, to hit verb 撞伤 (zhuàngshāng) injure by hitting, 撞坏 (zhuànghuài) wreck/damage by hitting 他不小心撞坏了自行车。 (Tā bù xiǎoxīn zhuàng huài le zìxíngchē.) He accidentally crashed and broke the bike. Use "撞伤" for people/animals, "撞坏" for objects.
咬 yǎo to bite verb 咬哭 (yǎokū) bite and make cry, 咬住 (yǎo zhù) hold tightly with teeth 小狗把他咬哭了。 (Xiǎo gǒu bǎ tā yǎo kū le.) The puppy bit him and made him cry. "咬哭" uses "哭" as a resultative complement.
打 dǎ to hit, to play (balls) verb 打破 (dǎpò) break/shatter, 打哭 (dǎkū) hit and make cry 那个小男孩把镜子打破了。 (Nàge xiǎo nánhái bǎ jìngzi dǎ pò le.) That little boy broke the mirror. "打破" is widely used metaphorically (e.g., break a record).
淋 lín to pour, to drench verb 淋湿 (línshī) soaked wet, 淋雨 (lín yǔ) get rained on 突然下雨，我的衣服被淋湿了。 (Tūrán xià yǔ, wǒ de yīfu bèi lín shī le.) It suddenly rained and my clothes got soaked. "淋湿" is the most common resultative pairing for rain.
抓 zhuā to catch, to grab verb 抓住 (zhuā zhù) catch hold of, 抓紧 (zhuā jǐn) hold tightly 猫抓老鼠很快。 (Māo zhuā lǎoshǔ hěn kuài.) The cat catches mice quickly. "抓紧" can metaphorically mean "to hurry up".
吹 chuī to blow, to boast verb 吹倒 (chuīdǎo) blow down, 吹牛 (chuīniú) boast 大风把外面的树吹倒了。 (Dàfēng bǎ wàimiàn de shù chuī dǎo le.) The strong wind blew down the tree outside. "吹倒" uses the directional/resultative "倒".
砍 kǎn to cut, to chop verb 砍倒 (kǎndǎo) chop down, 砍价 (kǎn jià) bargain 工人们正在砍倒那棵枯树。 (Gōngrénmen zhèngzài kǎn dǎo nà kē kū shù.) The workers are chopping down that dead tree. "砍价" is a highly common slang for haggling.
偷 tōu to steal; secretly verb, adverb 偷东西 (tōu dōngxi) steal things, 偷看 (tōu kàn) peek/steal a glance (secretly) 有人偷了我的钱包。 (Yǒu rén tōu le wǒ de qiңnbāo.) Someone stole my wallet.  
摔 shuāi to fall, to throw down verb 摔碎 (shuāisuì) shatter by dropping, 摔倒 (shuāi dǎo) fall down 他不小心把杯子摔碎了。 (Tā bù xiǎoxīn bǎ bēizi shuāi suì le.) He accidentally dropped and shattered the cup. "摔碎" implies the object broke into tiny pieces.
倒 dǎo to fall, to collapse verb, result complement 倒下 (dǎo xià) fall down, 倒塌 (dǎotā) collapse 书倒在地地上。 (Shū dǎo zài dìshàng le.) The book fell on the floor. Also pronounced "dào" when meaning "to pour".

破 pò broken, torn adjective, result complement 打破 (dǎpò) break/shatter, 破坏 (pòhuài) destroy 我的衣服被树枝挂破了。 (Wǒ de yīfu bèi shùzhī guà pò le.) My clothes were torn by a tree branch. Used for torn fabrics, paper, or punctured surfaces.
